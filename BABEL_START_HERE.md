## Core ecosystem rule

> **Product need earns an app. Naming/metaphor follows the job.**

Do not invent apps merely to complete an elemental set, populate Babel World, fill visual space, or create symmetry around FIRE.

A small ecosystem of excellent, genuinely distinct products is preferable to a complete-looking family of unnecessary apps.

Simple one-word names are preferred when they genuinely fit. Naming must not decide product scope.

## What is real now

### Babel FIRE — active product

FIRE is the production companion / decision engine.

Core promise:

> **What deserves my attention next, why, and what should I try in the DAW?**

FIRE takes musical material or a reference toward finished, release-ready audio. It has two first-class project types:

- **Original** — idea/material → finished record
- **Recreation** — reference → faithful cover, reconstruction, interpretation, reimagining, or production study

Recreation is **not** merely a learning mode. Someone may use FIRE only to make covers/recreations quickly and never use Original.

FIRE already owns contextual production learning. Its likely future optional **Practice / Skills / Growth** surface can provide deliberate training without turning FIRE into a mandatory course.

## Current surrounding-app concepts

### Dedicated learning product — not currently earned

There is no approved learning app, no approved name, and no requirement that one ever exist.

Current recommendation: keep contextual learning and optional deliberate practice inside FIRE unless deliberate training grows into a genuinely distinct product job — for example substantial production curriculum, ear-training laboratories, structured drills, blind diagnosis/A-B work, courses, or project-independent practice.

If a split is eventually earned, it should share genuinely overlapping production evidence through the future Babel Learning Contract while retaining its own local product identity.

**Important anti-drift rule:** do not simplify the ecosystem into:

> FIRE = creating. Learning app = studying.

That is false to the current product model. FIRE already contains Recreation, study, diagnosis, experimentation, contextual teaching, and optional practice.

Do **not** prematurely broaden the hypothetical learning product into a universal app for cinematography studies, rollout strategy, photography, live-show pacing, visual-design lessons, or every other creative discipline. Those may someday become separate legitimate jobs, but they are not currently defined here.

### Visual Identity product — promising candidate, deliberately underdefined

Visual identity is a genuinely promising separate product job to explore later.

Working job hypothesis:

> **Help turn the identity of an artist, era, song, or project into a coherent visual language.**

Possible areas include imagery, color, materials/textures, symbols, environments, typography, styling/clothing, lighting, photography/video language, artwork direction, stage/world atmosphere, and deciding what visually fits or does not fit.

These are boundaries for exploration, **not a locked feature list**. No final app name, element, workflow, or interface is chosen.

### Babel World — optional hub hypothesis

Babel World is the strongest current hub concept, but it is **not being built now** and is never required to use FIRE or another sibling app.

Potential future jobs include cross-app identity, meaningful shared progress/evidence views, badges/collections/discoveries, app handoffs, account/entitlement continuity, and an ecosystem-level view once multiple real products exist.

Guardrails:

- do not duplicate every app's task UI inside World
- do not create one global Babel skill/level score
- do not make World a required launcher/dependency
- do not invent sibling apps merely to give World content
- do not currently declare World the universal source of truth for every song/era/project object

The final collection/navigation form is intentionally unresolved. Babel has liked possibilities such as a **gallery, trophy case, map, neighborhood, park, path, or traversable world**.

> **The collection earns its shape.**

Do not design an empty world first and then invent products/rewards to populate it.

- WATER is **not** currently the Visual Identity app.

## Shared cross-app architecture that is preserved

Working principle:

> **Shared evidence, local identity.**

When two real Babel products genuinely observe the same capability, they should eventually share stable evidence semantics rather than invent incompatible meanings.

Important preserved ideas:

- future versioned **Babel Learning Contract**
- **evidence first, interpretation second**
- shared skill/domain identity only where meaning truly overlaps
- app-specific identity and progression metaphors remain local
- no universal Babel level
- source/origin marks and achievement badges are different semantics
- cross-app evidence requires real semantic overlap; app usage alone proves nothing

Do **not** confuse this with “every app edits the same project.” Shared song/project/era identifiers or objects may someday help, but that architecture is intentionally open. Each specialized app owns its task-specific data unless a real workflow proves sharing is useful.

Read `BABEL_APPS.md` and `BABEL_PLATFORM.md` before changing these semantics.

## Fire / local metaphors / pixel language

The user's evolving **Fire** is a strong future FIRE progression concept:

- truthful production-skill evidence underneath
- expressive, collectible progress artifact on top
- visibly changes/evolves over time
- can become more exciting and potentially more character-like within whatever pixel constraints eventually prove useful
- exact pixel dimensions, stages, animation, palette, and characterfulness remain open
- bigger never means objectively better producer

Another future product may have its own local metaphor. A **River** has been used only as an example of how a different app could have a different journey metaphor; it is not an assigned product/name.

In a far-future personal Babel World, different metaphors could coexist or visually interact without corrupting their semantics — for example, a River reflecting Fire light without mathematically leveling the Fire.

A deliberately original **pixel-art language** is a strong future hypothesis for Fire, source marks, app/domain identity marks, and achievement badges. Minecraft and classic Pokémon-era art are references for constrained readability, warmth, animation, and collectibility — never assets/styles to copy.

## Source marks, achievement badges, and collection

Keep the meanings separate:

1. **Source/origin marks** — provenance: which app(s) contributed credible evidence.
2. **Achievement badges** — meaningful accomplishments, milestones, discoveries, or genuinely cross-app achievements.
3. **App/domain identity marks** — identity for a real product/domain when useful; not automatically an achievement or skill claim.

Babel likes the future idea of one satisfying place in Babel World to browse accumulated badges/marks. Start with very few badges and implement them extremely well.

A meaningful achievement should have inspectable truth behind it: stable identity, criteria/meaning, provenance, and supporting evidence/milestone context where useful.

Do not assume a complete Fire/Water/Earth/Air badge set exists. Only products and achievements that actually earn existence should appear.

## Reference lenses worth remembering

Current useful references include Apple, Logic, Ableton, Basecamp/Shape Up, Duolingo, Google/Material, Figma, GitHub/Primer, ChatGPT, Things, Procreate, Snapchat, Instagram/TikTok observationally, Pokémon GO collection/spatial patterns, xAPI/xAPI Profiles, Open Badges 3.0, Minecraft, and classic Pokémon-era pixel-art qualities.

Particularly useful ecosystem lessons:

- **GitHub / Primer:** complex systems become learnable through stable vocabulary and hierarchy; naming is interface design.
- **ChatGPT:** expose only as much interface as the current job needs.
- **Google / Material + Figma:** shared semantics/components can support distinct products without making every app a skin.
- **Pokémon GO:** collection/spatial progression can work, but Babel World must earn its own form.
- **xAPI / xAPI Profiles:** store evidence/events + provenance first; derive skill state later.
- **Open Badges 3.0:** achievements should have inspectable meaning, not decorative emptiness.
