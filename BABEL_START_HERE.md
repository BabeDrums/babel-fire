## Core ecosystem rule

> **Product need earns an app. Naming/metaphor follows the job.**

Do not invent apps merely to complete an elemental set, populate Babel World, fill visual space, or create symmetry around FIRE.

A small ecosystem of excellent, genuinely distinct products is preferable to a complete-looking family of unnecessary apps.

Simple one-word names are preferred when they genuinely fit, but naming must not drive product scope.

## What is real now

### Babel FIRE — active product

FIRE is the production companion / decision engine.

Core promise:

> **What deserves my attention next, why, and what should I try in the DAW?**

FIRE takes musical material toward finished, release-ready audio. It has **two first-class project types**:

- **Original** — idea/material → finished record
- **Recreation** — reference → faithful cover, reconstruction, interpretation, reimagining, or production study

Recreation is **not** merely a learning mode. Someone may use FIRE only to make covers/recreations quickly and never use Original.

FIRE already owns contextual production learning. Its likely future optional **Practice / Skills / Growth** surface can provide deliberate training without turning FIRE into a mandatory course.

Do not create a separate learning app just because FIRE teaches people.

## App concepts that are NOT yet products

### Dedicated learning app — not currently earned

Current recommendation: **do not define or build one yet.**

A separate learning product becomes justified only if deliberate training grows into a genuinely distinct job that would make FIRE worse if kept inside it — for example, substantial curriculum, ear-training laboratories, structured drills, courses, or project-independent practice experiences.

If that split ever happens:

- it should consume the same future Babel Learning Contract / shared evidence model
- credible overlapping production evidence may inform the same production-skill model
- simply using the app must never grow the user's Fire
- its own local journey/metaphor may remain distinct

Do **not** prematurely redefine this as a broad app for studying cinematography, rollout strategy, photography, live-show pacing, or every creative discipline. Those may someday become valid jobs, but they have not been earned or defined here.

### Visual Identity app — promising candidate, deliberately underdefined

Babel has identified **visual identity** as a potentially distinct product job worth exploring after FIRE fundamentals are further along.

Rough job hypothesis:

> Help turn the identity of an artist, era, song, or project into a coherent visual language.

Possible concerns may eventually include things such as imagery, color, materials, symbols, environments, typography, photography/video language, styling, stage/world atmosphere, and what visually fits or does not fit.

These are brainstorming boundaries, **not a locked feature list or product spec**.

Important:

- no final app name or element is chosen
- do not call it Water/Air/Earth merely to complete a family
- do not assume it must share FIRE's interface
- determine whether the job truly deserves its own product before designing architecture around it

### Babel World — optional future hub hypothesis

Babel World is the strongest current hub concept, but it is **not being built now** and is not required for FIRE or any sibling app.

Potential future jobs include:

- cross-app identity
- meaningful progress/evidence views where aggregation is legitimate
- badges/collections/discoveries
- app handoffs
- entitlements/account-level continuity
- an ecosystem-level view once multiple real products actually exist

Guardrails:

- do not duplicate every app's task UI inside World
- do not create one global Babel skill/level score
- do not make World a required launcher or dependency
- do not invent sibling apps merely to give World content
- do not currently declare World the universal source of truth for every song/era/project object; that architecture remains open

The final collection/navigation form is intentionally unresolved. Babel has liked possibilities such as a **gallery, trophy case, map, neighborhood, park, path, or traversable world**. The collection should **earn its shape** from real apps, badges, and relationships rather than being designed empty first.

## Shared cross-app ideas that ARE preserved

Working principle:

> **Shared evidence, local identity.**

When two real Babel products genuinely observe the same capability, they should eventually share stable evidence semantics rather than inventing incompatible skill meanings.

Important preserved ideas:

- future versioned **Babel Learning Contract**
- **evidence first, interpretation second**
- shared skill/domain identity only where meaning truly overlaps
- app-specific product identity and progression metaphors remain local
- no universal Babel level
- source/origin marks and achievement badges are different semantics
- source marks answer where credible evidence came from
- achievement badges recognize meaningful accomplishments/discoveries/milestones
- cross-app evidence requires real semantic overlap; app usage alone proves nothing

Read `BABEL_PLATFORM.md` before changing these semantics.

## Fire / pixel language / badges

The user's evolving **Fire** is a strong future FIRE progression concept:

- truthful skill evidence underneath
- expressive, collectible progress artifact on top
- visibly able to change/evolve over time
- may become increasingly character-like/expressive within the limits of the chosen pixel system
- exact pixel dimensions, stages, animation, palette, and characterfulness are intentionally open

A deliberately original **pixel-art language** is also a strong future hypothesis for Fire, source marks, and achievement badges. Minecraft and classic Pokémon-era art are references for constrained readability/collectibility, not assets/styles to copy.

Babel World may eventually provide one satisfying place to browse accumulated app/domain/achievement badges. Its presentation remains open.
