## Core ecosystem rule

> **Product need earns an app. Naming/metaphor follows the job.**

Do not invent apps merely to complete an elemental set, fill Babel World, or create symmetry.

## What is real now

### Babel FIRE — active product

FIRE is the production companion / decision engine.

Core promise:

> **What deserves my attention next, why, and what should I try in the DAW?**

FIRE supports first-class Original and Recreation/Cover paths. Recreation is not merely a learning mode. FIRE already includes contextual diagnosis, experimentation, teaching, and likely optional deliberate practice without requiring a separate learning app.

## Current surrounding-product status

### Dedicated learning product — not currently earned

No approved learning app/name exists. Keep contextual learning and optional practice inside FIRE unless deliberate training becomes a genuinely distinct job that harms FIRE's primary production workflow when kept inside it.

Do not reduce the ecosystem to `FIRE = creating` / `learning app = studying`.

### Visual Identity product — promising candidate, deliberately underdefined

Working job hypothesis:

> **Help turn the identity of an artist, era, song, or project into a coherent visual language.**

This is a candidate product job, not an approved feature list/name/runtime.

### Babel World — optional future hub hypothesis

World may someday provide cross-app identity, meaningful shared progress/evidence views, collections/discoveries, handoffs, and account/entitlement continuity once multiple real products create a real hub job.

Guardrails:

- World is never required to use another Babel app
- do not duplicate each app's task UI inside World
- no universal Babel skill/level score
- do not invent apps/rewards just to populate World
- World is not currently the universal owner of every creative object

A **World prototype may live in Babel Lab**. Babel Lab itself remains the prototype sandbox and does not become the World product. A real World product earns its own repo later.

Current Lab ownership direction is more explicit:

- **Fire Lab** is the FIRE-bound visualization/progression experiment; promotion destination is Babel FIRE.
- **World Lab** is the World-bound experiment; Achievements live here.
- both may remain in the single `babel-lab` repository without implying they ship in one final product.

> **The collection earns its shape.**

## Babel Lab boundary

`BabeDrums/babel-lab` is the experimental sandbox/incubator.

Lab follows Babel Truth by default but may intentionally diverge when the experiment itself tests an alternative. Reconciliation must classify conflicts as:

- stale divergence
- intentional experiment
- potential upstream improvement
- app-local exception

Potential upstream improvements should be surfaced rather than silently overwritten.

## Elemental names remain unassigned

FIRE naturally creates elemental possibilities, but names do not create products.

- WATER is not currently the learning app
- WATER is not currently the Visual Identity app
- AIR/EARTH are not approved apps

If a real product earns existence first and an elemental metaphor later fits unusually well, naming can be reconsidered.

## Shared cross-app architecture preserved

Working principle:

> **Shared evidence, local identity.**

When real products genuinely observe the same capability, they should eventually share stable evidence semantics rather than invent incompatible meanings.

Preserve:

- future versioned Babel Learning Contract
- evidence first, interpretation second
- shared skill/domain identity only where meaning truly overlaps
- app-specific progression metaphors remain local
- no universal Babel level
- source/origin marks and achievement badges remain different semantics
- app usage alone proves nothing about skill

Do not interpret this as “every app edits one database/project.” App-specific working data stays local unless a real workflow earns sharing.

## Fire / achievements / pixel language

The user's evolving personal **Fire** remains a strong future FIRE progression concept:

- expressive artifact on top of truthful evidence
- visibly evolves over time
- bigger never means objectively better producer
- exact mapping, pixel dimensions, palette, stages, animation, and characterfulness remain open

Keep meanings separate:

1. source/origin marks = provenance
2. achievement badges = meaningful accomplishments/discoveries
3. app/domain identity marks = identity

A deliberately original pixel language remains a promising hypothesis for Fire, source marks, and badges; do not copy another game's assets/style.

## Functional terminology vs metaphor

Do not automatically rename musician-facing FIRE workflow concepts into fire-themed terminology merely for theme consistency.

Metaphor should clarify or delight, not force translation. Functional concepts such as Map / Structure / Tracks / Skeleton / Draft / Bounce keep musician-readable names unless a thematic alternative proves immediately clearer.

## Continuity

The old local `CONTINUITY_INBOX.md` is retired as an active buffer.

New unresolved durable ecosystem findings use **Babel Truth Issue #1** under Truth's persist-before-reply threshold.

Under the current append-safe Truth process:

- routine persist-before-reply capture is a **new Issue #1 comment**
- the Issue #1 body is the consolidated unresolved snapshot
- do not replace the full body during routine chat capture
- body rewrites happen only during an explicit consolidation pass after reading the current body and all newer comments

At consolidation:

- mature sibling-app job/boundary → `BABEL_APPS.md`
- mature shared architecture/progression semantics → `BABEL_PLATFORM.md` or Babel Truth when genuinely global
- current ecosystem routing/state → this file
- unresolved durable delta → Truth Issue #1 snapshot/comments until classified

Do not commit every brainstorm sentence.

## Parallel-chat rule

A FIRE build chat and Babel Lab/ecosystem chat may run in parallel.

Before durable writes, each chat must re-read:

- its repo's Truth pointer
- current local `main`
- open PRs
- relevant local authority
- the Truth-buffer body and relevant newer comments

If another chat already owns the same durable branch/file/PR, do not write from a stale snapshot; reconcile first or let the owning lane finish.

Lab/ecosystem work must not casually edit FIRE runtime/spec/data.
